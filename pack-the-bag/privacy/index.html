Privacy Notice: Pack the Bag

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Pack the Bag ("the game") is made by Picarol Games ("we", "us"). This notice explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers the version you play in your browser on itch.io.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. The only time we ask for your email is in the optional feedback form, if you want to hear when the full game comes out.
  • There are no ads and nothing to buy.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • If, and only if, you choose Share stats, the game sends anonymous play stats to Google Analytics: which levels you play and finish, and how long they take. We use them to make the levels better.
  • You can change your mind at any time in the game's Settings ▸ Share play stats.
  • If you fill in the feedback form, your answers reach us through Google Forms. It is up to you.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in your browser's storage. It is never sent to us:

  • your progress: the levels you have finished and their stars;
  • your settings: music and sound volumes, language, fullscreen, and your answer to the play stats question;
  • a log of your play sessions (what you did in each level and how long it took), which helps us when we test the game ourselves;
  • a random player id, made the first time you play. It is only sent anywhere if you choose Share stats (see below).

Clearing your browser's data for itch.io deletes all of it. Next time you play you start afresh, with a new random id.

Play stats (Google Analytics)

The first time you open the game, it asks whether you want to share play stats. Nothing is sent, and Google's code is not even loaded, until you choose Share stats. If you choose No thanks, nothing is ever sent.

If you choose Share stats, the game uses Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited (Google LLC outside Europe), to collect:

  • events in the game: the game opening; a level starting and ending, with its name, how it ended (finished or left), the stars, how long it took, and counts such as pieces moved, pieces turned and restarts; finishing every level; opening the feedback form or the game's itch.io page from the game;
  • the random player id saved by the game, so your sessions on different days count as one player;
  • information your browser sends: browser and operating system, device type, screen size and language, and the page the game is embedded in;
  • approximate location (country, region or city) derived from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store the IP address itself;
  • the date and time, and how long you played.

We set Google Analytics up so that it:

  • sets no cookies (the game keeps the random id itself);
  • does not use Google signals and does not use the data for advertising or ad personalisation;
  • keeps event data for 14 months and then deletes it automatically. After that we only have totals, such as how many players finished a level.

We use play stats only to understand how the game is played and improve it, for example to find a level that is too hard. We do not use them to identify you, and we do not sell them.

Google may process this information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Google handles it under its own privacy policy, policies.google.com/privacy, and you can read how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Feedback form (Google Forms)

The game's Feedback button opens a short form in a new browser tab. The form is run by Google Forms, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited (Google LLC outside Europe). Nothing is sent unless you fill it in and press Submit.

The form asks for:

  • your answers: how much you enjoyed the game, whether you would buy the full game, where you would rather play it, and what you liked or found confusing;
  • the game version, how many levels you finished and the game's language, which the game fills in for you so we know which version you played. You can see and change them before you submit;
  • if you want, your email address, so we can tell you when the full game comes out.

The form doesn't ask you to sign in and doesn't record your Google account, and your answers are not linked to your play stats or your random player id. Only we can read them; we do not sell or share them.

We use your answers only to improve the game and decide what to make next, and your email only to send you that one piece of news. We delete your email once we have sent it (or straight away if you ask), and we keep the other answers for at most two years. Google may process them outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards described above, and handles them under its privacy policy, policies.google.com/privacy.

itch.io

The game is hosted on itch.io, which runs the page you play it on. itch.io collects its own information about visits under its own privacy policy, itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy. We only see the totals it reports to developers, such as page views and plays.

Your choices and rights

  • You can switch play stats on or off at any time in Settings ▸ Share play stats. Switching them off stops anything more being sent; it does not affect what was sent before.
  • Clearing your browser's data for itch.io gives you a new random id, so new stats are no longer linked to old ones (it also clears your progress).
  • The feedback form is optional. To see, correct or delete what you sent there, including your email, write to us with roughly when you sent it and, if you left one, your email.
  • Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for play stats and for what you send in the feedback form, and storing your progress and settings on your device is needed for the game to work. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent. Because play stats are linked only to a random id, we usually can't tell which data is yours; contact us and we will help as far as we can. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

Children

Pack the Bag is for everyone. If you are under 14, or under the age at which you can consent on your own where you live, please ask a parent or guardian before choosing Share stats or leaving your email in the feedback form. If you believe a child has shared play stats or an email without that, contact us.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice, for example when the game comes to Steam or phones. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
