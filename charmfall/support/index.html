Charmfall Support

Help with the game, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Found a bug, have an idea or need help? Write to picarol.games+support@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? Your best score, game in progress and settings are on your device. Your coins, power-ups and daily streak are also kept on our server, with an anonymous account the game creates by itself.
  • I reinstalled the game or changed phone. Do I lose my coins? No. They come back by themselves: through Google Play Games on Android, and through your iPhone's Keychain or Game Center on iPhone. Your best score and game in progress stay on the old phone.
  • I paid but didn't get my coins. They arrive once the store confirms the payment. If you were offline or the game closed, they arrive the next time you open it with a connection. If they still don't, write to us with the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt.
  • How do I get a refund? Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store, and refunds are theirs to decide: ask through Google Play or Apple. What a refunded purchase gave is taken back.
  • How do I change my privacy choices? In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, open Privacy options in the game's settings. On iPhone you can also allow or stop tracking in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.
  • Why do I see ads? Ads keep Charmfall free. Videos are optional: you choose to watch one to get more power-ups.

Privacy and terms

Read how Charmfall handles your information in its privacy policy, and the rules for coins and purchases in its terms of use.
