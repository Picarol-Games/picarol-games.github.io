Privacy Policy: Charmfall

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Charmfall ("the app") is made by Picarol Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You don't need to sign up, and we don't ask for your email or contacts. The game gives you an anonymous account by itself, with a generated game name you can replace with one you type.
  • The weekly leaderboards and your profile show other players your game name, avatar, country flag, scores, best game and records. Friends you add see you on the Friends board.
  • Your best score, saved game and settings stay on your device. Your coins, power-ups and daily streak are kept on our server, so they can come back if you reinstall or change phone.
  • The game is free to play. It shows ads from Google AdMob, such as videos you can choose to watch to get more power-ups, and it sells optional coin packs and bundles through Google Play and the App Store. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, so we can improve it, and Firebase Crashlytics to learn when the game crashes or hits an error, so we can fix it.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your choice before ads are personalised or analytics and crash reports are collected, and you can change it at any time in the game's settings.
  • On iPhone, the game asks your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used to track you, and you can change your answer at any time in your iPhone's settings.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. We never receive it, apart from the number of games played and a summary of each finished game (see the next sections):

  • your best score;
  • the game in progress, so you can continue where you left off;
  • your settings (music, sound and vibration on or off);
  • when the app was installed or first launched, and how many games you have played (used to decide when the game starts showing a banner ad).

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device's settings, deletes all of it.

Your account, coins and power-ups

Your coins, power-ups and daily streak are kept on a server of ours that runs on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), services of Google LLC, in the United States. This is so they aren't lost with your phone, and so a purchase or a reward is given exactly once.

The first time you play, the app creates an anonymous account for you: a random identifier with no email attached. Its only name is the game name described under "Leaderboards, profile and friends". On the server we keep:

  • that identifier;
  • your balance of coins and power-ups, and your daily streak;
  • a record of what changed them (what was given, why and when), kept for 90 days;
  • the highest number of games played on the account, used to choose which progress to keep when two phones have played;
  • the link described below, which lets your progress come back.

To check that requests come from the real game, the app uses Firebase App Check, with Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple's App Attest on iPhone.

Getting your progress back. On Android, the game uses Google Play Games' Recall service: Google keeps a random token for us, linked to your Play Games profile, and we don't receive your Play Games name or email. On iPhone, the account stays in your phone's Keychain through a reinstall, and if you are signed in to Game Center we also keep your Game Center player identifiers (which Apple creates for each player), so that a new iPhone on the same Game Center account finds your progress.

We keep your account and balances so that your progress is there whenever you come back. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete them. To have them deleted, follow these steps: because the account has no email, we will ask for something that identifies it, such as your friend code or a purchase's order number.

Leaderboards, profile and friends

Charmfall has weekly leaderboards (World, your country and your friends) and a profile for every player. They are kept on the same server as your coins.

What other players see. Anyone playing Charmfall can see, on the leaderboards and on your profile:

  • your game name: one the game generates for you (such as "Lucky Clover 42"), or one you type;
  • your avatar (a charm and a colour);
  • your country's flag, which follows your phone's region setting;
  • your best score of each week and your rank;
  • your best game (its score, final board, length and stats, such as your biggest combo), your records, and your past weeks (the scores, ranks and rewards of the 12 most recent weeks you played, and totals such as your best rank).

Other players don't see your coins, your purchases or your list of friends.

Your name. A name you type is 3 to 16 characters, and our server checks it against a word filter before anyone sees it. Other players see it, so don't use your full name or other personal details. The first time you type a name, you accept the terms of use, and we record which version you accepted and when.

Your games. When a game ends, the app sends our server a summary of it: an identifier for the game, when it ended, the score, the number of pieces placed, the stage reached, how long it took, its stats (such as charms cleared and the biggest combo) and the final board. The server checks that the score is possible, then updates your best score of the week, your best game and your records. It remembers the identifiers of your last 200 games, so a game sent twice counts once.

Friends. Every player has a friend code. You can share it, or an invite link that holds it. Anyone who opens the link sees a web page with your game name, avatar, flag, best score and friend code. On Android, when someone installs the game from that page, Google Play passes the code to the game. When someone adds you with your code, you become friends both ways and see each other on the Friends board. We keep a friendship until one of you removes or blocks the other.

Reports and blocks. If you report a player, we keep the report: who sent it, who it is about, the reason, the name it was about, and the date. We use reports only to moderate names. If we replace a name that broke the rules, we keep the old name and the date on that account, and after the second time the account can no longer type a name. If you block a player, we keep that on your account so the app can hide them from you, until you unblock them.

Weekly rewards. When a week closes, the server ranks every player's best score of the week and adds the reward for your place to your coins and power-ups. It keeps a record of your place and reward for 12 weeks, so the game can show it to you.

How long we keep it. The weekly scores and leaderboards are kept for 8 weeks (the current week and the seven before it) and then deleted. Your profile, best game, records, past weeks, friends and blocks are kept as long as your account exists. Reports are deleted 90 days after they are made, or sooner if the account that sent them, or the one they are about, is deleted. Deleting your data removes all of it, including the reports you sent and those about you.

Where the GDPR applies, we use this information to provide the leaderboards, profile and friends you use in the game (performance of a contract), and to keep names and scores fair and safe for every player (our legitimate interest).

Purchases

Charmfall sells optional coin packs and bundles. Google Play or Apple's App Store handles the payment, under its own terms and privacy policy, and we never see your card or payment details.

When you buy, the store gives the app a purchase token or a signed transaction. The app sends it to our server, which checks it with the store and gives you what you bought, once. We keep a record of each purchase: your account identifier, the store, the product and quantity, what you were given, the store's order number and the date. We keep it for as long as we need it to handle refunds and chargebacks and to meet our tax and accounting duties.

If a purchase is refunded, the store tells us and we take back what it gave you, stopping at zero. After two refunds on an account, we pause purchases on it until you contact us.

Firebase Analytics records purchases by itself (the product, its price and currency, the date). The app reaches the stores through Unity's In-App Purchasing library, which sends Unity technical information about how the library is working.

Where the GDPR applies, we keep your account and purchase records to provide the game and what you bought (performance of a contract), to meet our tax and accounting duties (legal obligation) and to prevent fraud and abuse (our legitimate interest).

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland), for example videos you can choose to watch to get more power-ups. Ads are how the game stays free.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, AdMob collects from your device:

  • your device's advertising identifier: the Android advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA, but only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • an app-instance identifier and, on iPhone, Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version, network type);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • how you interact with the ads (ads shown, taps, whether a video was watched to the end).

Where you agree, or where your region doesn't require consent, this information can be used to personalise the ads you see. If you don't agree, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

We don't receive your identity from AdMob, only reports such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned. With your consent, AdMob also reports the ads you see, and what they earned, to our Firebase Analytics (see below).

When you watch a rewarded video, AdMob also tells our server, with your account identifier, that it was watched, so the reward is given once. We keep that record for 90 days.

Read how Google uses information for advertising and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Tracking on iPhone

On iPhone, the first time you open the app, after the European consent form where it applies, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether Charmfall may track you. Google's short explanation may appear just before it. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads and to measure them across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you ask the app not to track, the IDFA is not shared: you still see ads, but they are not personalised using it.

You can change your answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.

Your consent in Europe

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the app it shows a consent form from Google, based on the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Your answers decide whether information may be stored on and read from your device, whether ads may be personalised, and which ad partners may process your data. We only turn on Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics if you agree to information being stored on your device.

You can change your choices at any time from Privacy options in the game's settings.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for analytics, for crash reports, for storing and reading information on your device and for personalised ads. Non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free, as described in the consent form, where you can also object to it.

Information collected through Firebase Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to collect usage information, such as:

  • events in the game (for example, a run started or ended, a power-up used, the score reached);
  • ad events (an ad shown, a video watched to the end, the revenue it earned, an ad that failed to load);
  • purchases (the product, its price and currency);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size);
  • an app-instance identifier and, where your device allows it, its advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA only if you allowed tracking);
  • approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address;
  • the date and time and how long the app was used.

We use this information only to understand how players use the game, find problems, improve it and see how the ads perform. We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not sell it.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

Crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to tell us when the game crashes or runs into an error, so we can find the cause and keep the game working on every phone. When that happens, Crashlytics sends a report with:

  • the error or crash itself: what went wrong and where in the game's code (a stack trace);
  • device and app information at that moment (device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and disk space, whether the app was in the foreground, screen orientation);
  • a Crashlytics installation identifier, a random identifier for this installation that tells us how many players a problem affects, and the Firebase installation ID;
  • a short trail of what the game was doing just before: the screen that was open, and when a game started and ended with the game's random identifier, so a crash can be matched to that game in our analytics;
  • the game settings from Remote Config that were active at the time.

Crash reports don't include your name, an account, your contacts or your location; the identifiers above are random and belong to this installation of the game.

Crashlytics follows the same choice as analytics. If you have not agreed, or have withdrawn your agreement, reports are not sent; the app keeps them on your device, and sends them only if you agree later. We use crash reports only to fix problems in the game, and we do not sell them.

Google keeps crash reports and their identifiers for 90 days and then deletes them. You can read how Firebase handles this data at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Game settings from Firebase Remote Config

The app downloads a few game settings from Firebase Remote Config, a service provided by Google LLC, so we can adjust the game (for example, from which point a banner ad is shown) without an app update. To do this it sends a Firebase installation ID, a random identifier for this installation that does not identify you or your device, together with basic app and device information (app version, operating system version, language, country and time zone).

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can change your consent choices, analytics and crash reports included, at any time from Privacy options in the game's settings.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads).
  • On iPhone, you can allow or stop tracking at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.
  • You can change your game name and avatar on your profile, and unblock players in the game's settings. Your country follows your phone's region setting.
  • You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the app. What our server already holds stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Charmfall is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
