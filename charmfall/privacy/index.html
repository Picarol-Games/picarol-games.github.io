Privacy Policy: Charmfall

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Charmfall ("the app") is made by Picarol Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress (best score, saved game, settings) stays on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, so we can improve it.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. We never receive it:

  • your best score;
  • the game in progress, so you can continue where you left off;
  • your settings (music, sound and vibration on or off);
  • a record of when the app was first launched.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

Information collected through Firebase Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to collect anonymous usage information, such as:

  • events in the game (for example, a run started or ended, a power-up used, the score reached);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size);
  • an app-instance identifier and, where your device allows it, the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address;
  • the date and time and how long the app was used.

We use this information only to understand how players use the game, find problems and improve it. We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not sell it.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads).
  • You can stop all collection at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Charmfall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features such as ads. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
