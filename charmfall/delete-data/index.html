Delete your data: Charmfall

Charmfall by Picarol Games. Last updated: 31 October 2026

Charmfall has no sign-up. It keeps your coins, power-ups and daily streak on our server, under an anonymous account the game creates by itself. You can ask us to delete that data at any time.

How to ask

  1. Write to picarol.games+pp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Charmfall data".
  2. Include the order number from a Google Play or Apple receipt for Charmfall. The account has no name or email, so this is how we find it.
  3. We delete the data and confirm by email within one month.

If you have never bought anything in Charmfall, nothing links the account to you, so we can't find it to delete it. Uninstalling the game stops all collection and deletes what is on your phone.

What we delete

  • your anonymous account and its identifier;
  • your balance of coins and power-ups, and your daily streak, including anything you bought;
  • the record of what changed them, and of the rewarded videos you watched;
  • the link that brings your progress back through Google Play Games or Game Center.

This can't be undone: the coins and power-ups can't be brought back afterwards.

What we keep

We keep the record of each purchase (the account identifier, the store, the product and quantity, what it gave, the store's order number and the date) for up to six years, to handle refunds and chargebacks and to meet our tax and accounting duties. We then delete it.

Analytics data and crash reports aren't linked to this account. Google deletes them by itself, after 2 months and 90 days. Read more in the privacy policy.
