Delete your data: Charmfall

Charmfall by Picarol Games. Last updated: 6 November 2026

Charmfall has no sign-up. It keeps your coins, power-ups and daily streak, and your profile, scores and friends for the leaderboards, on our server, under an anonymous account the game creates by itself. The account has no email: its only name is your game name. You can ask us to delete that data at any time.

How to ask

  1. Write to picarol.games+pp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Charmfall data".
  2. Include your friend code, or the order number from a Google Play or Apple receipt for Charmfall. This is how we find the account. Your friend code is on your profile (tap your avatar) and on the Friends tab of RANKS.
  3. We delete the data and confirm by email within one month.

If you can't find a friend code in the game and have never bought anything in Charmfall, nothing links the account to you, so we can't find it to delete it. Uninstalling the game stops all collection and deletes what is on your phone.

What we delete

  • your anonymous account and its identifier;
  • your balance of coins and power-ups, and your daily streak, including anything you bought;
  • the record of what changed them, and of the rewarded videos you watched;
  • the link that brings your progress back through Google Play Games or Game Center;
  • your profile: your game name, avatar and country, your best game, your records and your past weeks;
  • your friend code, and your scores and rows on the weekly leaderboards;
  • your friends, on both sides: you leave their Friends board too;
  • the players you blocked, and other players' blocks of you;
  • the reports you sent and the reports about you;
  • the record of your weekly rewards, of any name we replaced, and of your acceptance of the terms of use.

This can't be undone: the coins and power-ups can't be brought back afterwards.

What we keep

We keep the record of each purchase (the account identifier, the store, the product and quantity, what it gave, the store's order number and the date) for up to six years, to handle refunds and chargebacks and to meet our tax and accounting duties. We then delete it.

AdMob's confirmation of each rewarded video (the account identifier and when it was watched) stays until it is deleted automatically, 90 days after the video.

Analytics data and crash reports aren't linked to this account. Google deletes them by itself, after 2 months and 90 days. Read more in the privacy policy.
